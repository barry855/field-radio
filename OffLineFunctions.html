⚡ FIELD RADIO — OFFLINE FUNCTIONS

What still works with no internet connection (e.g. radios only, no Wi-Fi/data)
Big picture: Field Radio's peer-to-peer connection itself requires internet — it relies on a signaling server (PeerJS) and presence service (Firebase) to find the other device, plus STUN/TURN servers to establish the audio/video/data link. Without internet, ALPHA and BRAVO cannot connect to each other. Everything below applies to a single device, already-open instance of the app — useful for prep, review, or if you opened the page before losing signal.

⚠️ Partially Works / Device-Dependent

StatusFeatureNotes
~TALK (speech-to-text dictation)On Chrome, speech recognition typically requires a network connection to Google's servers — may fail or show "NOT SUPPORTED" offline. Some browsers/OS combos support fully local recognition.

Practical tip: If you're prepping before heading somewhere without signal, open both pages, save your speech/translate language preferences, and write/save any draft messages while you still have a connection — those are stored locally and will be there when you reconnect.
