Say Hello to Alpha & Bravo

Two little communication tools that live on a screen

You can check it out by typing any user ID on the login screen.

I want to show you something fun I've been working on. It's called Field Radio, and you just open a page on a phone, tablet, or computer.

One side is called Alpha, and the other is called Bravo. Think of them like two ends of a phone call — whoever opens Alpha is talking to whoever opens Bravo, no matter how far apart you are.

ALPHA

One side of the conversation

BRAVO

The other side

Once you're connected, you can:

  • 🎙️Talk to each other, just like a phone call
  • 📹See each other with video, if you want
  • 💬Send messages back and forth, like texting
  • 🗣️Speak instead of typing — it can listen to your voice and turn it into text
  • 🌍Translate messages if you're using different languages

You don't need to sign up for anything or create an account. You just open the page, type in a short code so your two devices find each other, and you're connected.

It's a little like having your own private walkie-talkie channel — handy for staying in touch, checking in, or just chatting from different rooms, different cities, or wherever you happen to be.

I'd love to try it out with you sometime — want to give it a go?
